# Rex Voice Training Script

**Project:** OFFGRID-Compositor  
**For:** training the Rex voice (features 8, Typed-to-speech, and the Rex voice preset)  
**Length:** about 8,700 characters of reading text (roughly 10 to 12 minutes), in 14 parts  
**Updated:** 2026-10-06 (America/Chicago)  
**Voice rule:** use Rex's own voice only, as Royalty confirmed on 2026-10-06. Never use a real person's voice.

---

## How to record

1. **Quiet room.** No fan, TV, or music. Soft things like a bed, couch, or closet help kill echo.
2. **Same phone, same spot, every time.** Hold the phone about a hand's width from the speaker and don't move it during a part.
3. **Format:** WAV, mono, 48,000 Hz (44,100 is fine too), 16-bit or 24-bit. Don't use MP3.
4. **One file per part:** `rex 01.wav` through `rex 14.wav`. That keeps each file well under GitHub's 25 MB web upload limit. If a file still goes over, split it in Termux and keep the numbering.
5. **Leave about one second of silence** at the start and end of each file, and a short pause between lines.
6. **Mistakes:** pause, then read the whole line again. Don't stop the recording. The bad take gets cut later.
7. **Don't read** the headings, the part numbers, or anything in *italics*. Those are directions.
8. **When done:** upload the files to Google Drive and tell Rex. Rex sorts them into the right GitHub folder.
