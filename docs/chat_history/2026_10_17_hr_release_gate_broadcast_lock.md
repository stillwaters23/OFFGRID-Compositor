## Defects found and fixed

1. Back exits the app while locked. Fix: `lockBackCallback`, enabled from `onLockApplied` and at startup. Tests: `LockGuardsTest.backIsSwallowedForEveryLockSource`, `backIsFreeWhenUnlocked`, `theShareEndingDoesNotUnlockWhileArmed`.
2. Camera permission dialog opens over a locked stage. Fix: `askCameraPermission` waits for the unlock and is called again from `onLockApplied`. Tests: `LockGuardsTest.noPermissionDialogWhileLockedForAnySource`.
3. A two-finger hold started locked can lock again when the lock changes under the fingers. Fix: `holdOutcome`. Tests: `LockGestureMapTest.aHoldMeansWhatItMeantWhenTheFingersLanded`.
4. Extra gesture-map coverage (corners, edge finger, cancel). Tests: `everyCornerAndEdgeOfTheStageRejectsALockStart`, `anUnlockHoldStartsLikeALockHoldAndKeepsHoldingStill`.

## What earlier agents got wrong or missed

- Builder and QA: missed the Back key (the panel's back callback goes off with the panel) and the camera permission dialog (QA gated only the mic dialog). Neither looked at the lock changing during a two-finger hold.
- Prior HR auditor: left leads 6 and 7 open; both led to real defects. Its leads 1 to 5, 8 and 9 held up.
- Docs claim "tests cover REMOTE lock and disarm in the live module". They cover the message format only; the behavior is covered in the store tests.

## Issue #45 (stacked on this branch)

1. Pairing screen-share guard. Fix and tests named in the `PairingActivity` row above. Tests: `PairingSharePolicyTest` (`thePolicyNumbersMatchThePlatformConstants`, `aVisibleShareOnAndroid15ClosesPairing`, `notVisibleLeavesPairingOpen`, `belowAndroid15DoesNothingEvenWhenTheStateSaysVisible`, `theGuardIsRegisteredOnlyByPairing`).
2. Camera permission is asked once, only after the lock lifts. `LockGuards.cameraPrompt` (`lock_guards.kt:21`) returns nothing while locked, before resume, or when permission is already granted. A store notification may launch the one deferred ask and never calls `showPermissionHelp`. `MainActivity.followCameraPrompt` (`MainActivity.kt:334`) is the only caller, from `onLockApplied` (`:310`) and `onResume` (`:326`). Tests: `LockGuardsTest.coldStartWhileTheShareIsVisibleAsksOnceAfterTheLockLifts`, `resumeAfterBackgroundingWhileLockedDoesNotAskOrShowHelp`, `aFreshInstallWhileUnlockedAsksOnce`, `cameraPermissionAlreadyGrantedNeverAsks`, `everyLockSourceHoldsTheCameraPrompt`.
3. A two-finger unlock swallows the rest of the gesture. `LockGestureMap.swallowsRest` (`lock_gesture_map.kt:46`) is true for `LOCK` and `UNLOCK`. `LockTouch.fireLock` (`broadcast_lock_touch.kt:111`) sets `swallowRest` and cancels the views for both. Test: `LockGestureMapTest.anUnlockHoldSwallowsTheRestOfTheGestureTheSameWayALockDoes`.
4. `TerminalPanel.kt:391-393` no longer says Phone 2 can reopen a shrunk panel. No fire word does that. Test: `RemoteLockMessageTest.phone2HasNoCommandThatReopensThePanel`. Historical chat notes were not rewritten.

Known limits, not passes: below API 35 there is no official callback, so the guard does nothing (covered by the unit test). A key already copied to the clipboard before the share starts is still on the clipboard until the existing 60-second clear. If `addScreenRecordingCallback` throws `SecurityException`, Pairing stays open. A real Bigo share on the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is BLOCKED (phone only).
